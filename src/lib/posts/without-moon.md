_Without moon_

---

there's a people who're fools, without their moon.

now we tell a tale of the moon, for fools without moons (that's you, as your clue).

---

without moon we don't bloom

our hearts lost without moon

---

for our blooming, our moon

yielding our fuel

---

our moon, the one for fuel

gone without clue

---

now we're lost without moon

why would the moon?

---

the moon, a tool for our fools

a moon tired of fools

---
a time when the moon

left without clue

---

in time, desperation blooms

---

desperate

now yielding crown for clue

---

...

---
by the moon!

a clue for us fools

---

the seeking fools

clueless of truth

---

desperate for moon

respect is due

---
in time with a clue

time to rendevouz with the moon

---

...

---
the moon is back

our moon has our back!

---

but not without pact

---
a price for our moon

a fool for the moon

---
the fool's on the moon

for without our moon 

we have not our boon

---
_closes book._

and for the time of the fool

read lost on the moon 

---
_Lost on the moon_

---

lost on the moon

a faithless fool


in times without tool

mirthless and blue


seeks not where's the tool

for he's lost on the moon

 

and in time he cools

faithless, still fool


in time the cool body of a fool

still on the moon


in time the spirit of the fool

has departed the moon
