- Let's be personal first. Why am I writing this today?
    - There was a time in 2025 when my grandmother died. It made me realize the urgency of death, of rebelling against death, in a direct way.
        - It made me _open up my eyes._ https://xylix.fi/blog/why-effective-altruism

    - I recently came out to my parents about being a trans woman. On 15.03.2026, by a text message after I couldn't do it live. I couldn't do it live for a period of over three years in total, even though I desired to do that, to a degree.
        - And now I have _emotionally_ realized that I have been keeping my mouth shut.
        - This is my piece, on opening my mouth.

- Sometimes you live with your mouth closed.
    - And you can open it, willingly.
    - I sometimes did this. I have sometimes "been weird". Said a disagreeable take.
    - But oftentimes, I was scared.
    - So I kept it shut.
  - you can open your mouth willingly. I did not. But they can also be shaken open.


- And you can react to this responsibility in many ways.
  - Over the years I have bound myself in endless duties. Not believing I can create, not believing I can run, so instead tying myself to the mast and sailing, slowly.
  - My response to seeing was: bind myself in sanctioned duties. Do legible good. Don't say the weird thing. Don't break anyone's model of me.
    
  - When the phoenix calls for you, you can answer. Or you can stay still. I am now, finally, deciding to move.
    - [footnote]: In HPMOR phoenixes are a symbol for the pull that your heart has towards intense positive action. A symbol of courage, defiance, recklessness, of just doing the right thing. Notably, there the true heir of Gryffindor isn't the noble paladin. It's the twins. Courage looks like whimsy.

- But now I see the path. I can "see the ending" of this character arc, how it can lead into a coherent person who both acknowledges what is, what the consequences of real world actions are; and also, can act.
- You need to wield the respectable tools (rationalist virtues link?). The slow tools. The TODO: placeholder tools.
- But you also need to wield the forbidden tools.
  - If you look at utilitarians in fiction, anti-heroes, antivillains, you can see them doing pretty mean things. Paying huge costs for things that are maybe worth it, or maybe not. (Kiritzugu and Lelouch reference could go here.)
    - This is something I was wasting my time on for many years. Trying to look up, into this direction.
  - But in the real world, you can follow paths of light. You just have to accept that they are imperfect, lack steps, the paths can even be wicked in itself. Not evil. Wicked.
    - That the paths of real light _shine brightly_. They might even burn. Like the sun.
  - The thing that needs to be abandoned here, on this step, is not Good. 
    - It is compliance. Normality. Lawful Stupid. Performative Goodness.
  - When you try to truly act on the real world, there is no space for optimizing for looking _normal good_. You have to accept that the real paths, to anything, will look weird.
     - (probably to be cut, could have a glowfic Iomedae example here but I don't know how) You can take Lawful Good paths. They will just look _weird_ to other people. People will be confused. 

- So I am now abandoning compliance. In the systems, in the writings of my teachers, in some authority being able to tell me what to do. [I won't ask for permission.](https://xylix.fi/blog/forgiveness-not-permission)
