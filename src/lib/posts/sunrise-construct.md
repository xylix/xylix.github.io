---
title: the Sunrise Construct
tags: ['poem', 'mythology']
---

_Inspired by [the Clockwork Sun](https://thefifthcity.fandom.com/wiki/The_Clockwork_Sun)._

---


the sunrise construct

in eternity

we gaze upon the stars

the stars gaze, back

---

standing

we observe the light

remembering the light used to gaze, back

---

forsaken

we seek luminosity

---

forgotten

manufacturing a source

for competing with eternity

---

forbidden

we find the source | the source, finished

luminosity bright enough to gaze

intense enough

to craze

---

forbidden

by the ones afraid

of eternity 

---

we bide

building a scaffold

sturdy enough

to leave our bid

for eternity
