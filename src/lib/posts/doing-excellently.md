Doing things excellently is [hard](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2017/08/26/play-in-hard-mode/). You should do excellent work, in part because it is hard, to become stronger. Why excellent? Because perfect is the enemy of good.

I have taken to using expressions in the manner of "we should strive for _excellent_ results" as a shorthand for the pretty entangled concept of - doing things actually well.

So let's untangle the concept, what does _excellent_ mean here?

Vibes:
- Significantly better than the "bare minimum"
- But less rigid than "perfect"
- Aiming for the correct success criteria - see [hard mode](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2017/08/26/play-in-hard-mode/). No Goodhearting, when taking shortcuts being mindful what the compromises are.
    - TODO: The hard mode form example (number VI?) is relevant here: I think > (quote) is looking at the right direction, but taking into account [forgiveness over permission](/blog/forgiveness-not-permission), usually the correct habit to cultivate is being mindful of what information is relevant and filling that in honestly and correctly and with proper diligence.
- An excellent work output is tangibly and noticeably excellent when observed.

Examples:
- Creating an excellent dish: drawing from either experience or a reasonably chosen recipe, paying attention into your estimates of the tastes of the target audience (be it you, someone else, or the internet), making reasonable price - quality tradeoffs in ingredient selection, and cooking this into something great.
- How to differentiate between a good vs. excellent blog post?
    - A good post has to: Have reasonable text quality, say something, say it understandably, 
    - An excellent post has to: Have all of the above, and go through editing rounds,
