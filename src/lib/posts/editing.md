## Target
- I don't silently edit substantive claims or arguments.
    - Exception: Posts that are drafts may be subject to major revisions.

## Redactions:
- Redactions are findable in the blogs git history https://github.com/xylix/xylix.github.io. Every blog post's _Revisions_ section at the bottom links to its git history.
- I reserve the right to redact parts of old posts, for these reasons and others of similar magnitude: 
    - I think there is a good reason to avoid linking an old source
    - My estimate of the source's reliability has changed significantly
    - Privacy
